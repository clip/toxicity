Where the messages come from

Written set. Hand-written threads based on patterns people commonly post about on relationship forums like r/relationship_advice. They aren't copied from real people's messages.

Remixes. The game builds new threads from parts: real-feeling situations, tactics, names, timestamps and read receipts. That's thousands of combinations, all free, and every remix is the same for everyone so comments and votes stick to it.

Player texts. Anyone can add a text in Crowd Verdict. There's no answer key, so the crowd decides.

Reddit. When a scenario is based on a specific Reddit post, a "From Reddit" link appears with it.

What the game saves

The game gives your browser a random anonymous ID, so your votes, comments and history follow you on this device. No account, email or location is collected. To improve the game it saves your scores and answers in each mode, your feedback ratings, and whether you're on a phone or computer; only the game's owner sees those, as anonymous totals. Votes, comments, your chosen name and texts you add are shared with other players. Clearing your browser data starts you over with a new ID.

If a text in your real life looks like a 9 or 10

Threats, demands for your location or passwords, and threats to share private photos are serious. Save the messages and talk to someone you trust. In the US you can reach the loveisrespect helpline by texting LOVEIS to 22522 or calling 1-866-331-9474.
